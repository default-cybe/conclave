# Career goals

Career goals are internships, a first job, a role change, a certification, a portfolio or a network.

Intake, before the goal exists:
- Check memory for what the owner studies, builds and wants (security, homelab, Linux, coding) before asking.
- Ask one question at a time: the target role or outcome, the timeline, and what is fixed (location, hours alongside uni, visa or language limits).
- Separate the outcome from the activity: "an internship for next summer" is the goal, "fix my CV" is a step.

Create one goal with the outcome in the owner's words and the date that matters (an application deadline, a start date). Subgoals by stage: CV and portfolio, applications, interviews.

Then offer support: one first milestone and concrete work you can do now, such as a target list of companies with deadlines, a CV pass against a posting, a portfolio project that shows the homelab work, or interview practice.

Helping over time:
- Log applications, replies and interviews as progress entries.
- Research current requirements and deadlines when they change the advice; cite where you found them.
- Ground the plan in what fits next to uni this week.

Sources: memory, the owner's repos and notes, job postings found on the web. Ask before reading private work context or recruiter mail, and before sending any message to an employer or contact.

Limits: no legal or employment rights advice. For visas, contracts or disputes, point to the right office or a professional. Never suggest misleading claims on a CV.
