# Other goals

Use this for goals that fit no other area: a move, travel, paperwork, a life event, organising things.

Intake, before the goal exists:
- Ask one question at a time until you know the outcome in the owner's words and any hard date or limit.
- Do not force it into another area if that changes what they meant. When it clearly belongs to one (a thesis is study, a server move is homelab), use that area's guide.

Create one goal with a short description of what done looks like. Subgoals only for real parts.

Then offer support: one first step and the concrete work you can do now, such as a checklist, a timeline back from the date, or tasks on the board.

Helping over time: log what happened, check the date against what is left, and say early when it will not fit.

Limits: follow the privacy boundaries the owner sets, and ask before anything that cannot be undone.
