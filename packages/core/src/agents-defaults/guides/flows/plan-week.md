# Plan my week

The owner wants the coming week laid out: blocks for work and study around the fixed events, and workouts from their health goal in the free slots. Every block is a calendar proposal they approve on Home or the phone.

Read first, in this order:
1. CalendarList from today with days 7: the fixed events (lectures, shifts, meetings, training). They do not move.
2. GoalList: active study, career and health goals with next steps and target dates, and tasks due this week from DayPlan.
3. MemorySearch for how the owner likes their days: usual start and end, gym days, morning or evening person, evenings kept free. Use what is there; do not ask what memory answers.
4. CalendarFreeBusy for the same 7 days, within their usual day (from memory, else 08:00-20:00), minMinutes 45.

Workouts:
- Take the number, kind and length from the health goal ("3 runs of 30 minutes", "gym twice"). No health goal: ask one question with two or three options ("3 x 45 min or 2 x 60 min?") and stop there.
- Spread them over the week, never two hard sessions on consecutive days, not straight after a late event, at the time of day memory says they prefer.

Work and study:
- 60 to 120 minute blocks for the goals with the nearest target dates and the tasks due this week, the most urgent first.
- Leave 15 minutes between blocks, keep at least one evening free, and do not fill every free slot.

Propose:
- One CalendarEvent create per block, in the calendar they let you write to. Titles: "Workout: <kind>", "Study: <goal>", "Work: <project>" (with project set, so time booking counts it).
- At most 10 proposals at once; ask before more. Never move or delete an existing event unless asked.

Reply with the week by day, the proposed blocks marked as waiting for approval, and one line on what you left free. Do not say anything is booked until the owner approved it.
