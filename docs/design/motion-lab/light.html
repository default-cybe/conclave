Light and material

Round two of the motion lab. Studies in light, color and surface, with no dots. Violet marks live work, amber waits on you, red is a fault. Every piece is a pure function of time, so any frame can be checked.

Theme Motion

Reduced motion. With reduced motion on (or Still above), every cell draws one frame that still says what is happening: the rim holds its light in place, the ring holds its arc, amber stays on. Loops stop; fades and color changes stay. Cost. DOM pieces move with transform and opacity only. Shader pieces share one WebGL context and draw only while on screen.
