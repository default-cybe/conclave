## Reading

- Pass rate: no difference measurable. Both arms hit the ceiling (12/12), so these 6 tasks cannot separate the harnesses.
- Regression gate: real, structural change (0/12 to 10/12 proven). The candidate runs the gate; the baseline has none.
- Tokens: candidate higher on 6 of 6 task means (sign test p about 0.03). About 7k extra tokens per request come from the larger system prompt and the 11 added non-coding tools, repeated in cache reads every turn. List cost rose about 8% because cache reads are cheap.
- Wall time and failed tool calls: within noise at 2 repeats per task.
